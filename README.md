# BEU ECE 4th Semester PYQ Archive

> Minimalist, mobile-first previous year question paper archive and AI solver for Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna.

Crafted in **Nothing Tech Minimalist Style** (Monochrome • Dot-matrix aesthetic • Atyp typography).

---

## ⚡ Features
- 📱 **Mobile & Laptop Responsive:** Optimized for smartphones (OLED-friendly dark mode) and wide desktop screens.
- ✦ **AI Question Solver:** Every question includes a 1-tap `[ ✦ ASK AI ]` button with integrations for ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude AI, and DeepSeek, plus clipboard prompt copy and mobile system share.
- 📐 **Vector Math & Circuits:** Complete MathJax LaTeX formulas and embedded official circuit diagrams.
- 📥 **Direct PDF Downloads:** All papers and master compilations hosted on Catbox CDN.
- 🎯 **25 VVI Question Packs:** Dedicated curated high-yield question packs for every subject.
